Enterprise Manufacturing KPI Intelligence System

A production-grade BI platform deployed across 3 plants — used in COO-level daily reviews at Aliaxis Ashirvad

The Problem

Before this system existed, plant KPI reporting was fragmented across six disconnected data sources — SAP, IoT sensors, SharePoint lists, and multiple Excel files maintained manually by different departments. Stakeholders spent 12+ hours per week consolidating data into slides. There was no single source of truth, no standardized KPI definitions, and leadership decisions were based on data that was sometimes days old. When the analyst was absent, reporting stopped entirely.
